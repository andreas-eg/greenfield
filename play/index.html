Greenfield

Stockholm skate, on the sk8 simulation
Controls
PadLeft stick lean and spin, right stick is the board: pull back, flick forward to ollie, curve to flip. A push, B brake, X mongo push, Y off board, triggers grab, both triggers + both stick clicks bail.
KeysW/A/S/D left stick (W pushes), S+A/D powerslide, mouse is the right stick (click to lock), Space push, C brake, E mongo, F off board, mouse buttons grab, Q/R bumpers, Shift L3, Tab menu.
TouchLeft half: a floating stick, tap to push. Right half: the board stick, swipe up to ollie. Corner buttons grab.
